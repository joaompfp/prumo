## Original visual direction (superseded colours only)

A restrained editorial workspace: warm paper, white working surfaces, dark green ink and petrol-green primary actions. Brass is a secondary detail, not an additional status colour. A simple vertical string and pointed plumb weight form the brand mark; it must read at favicon size. Keep the public editorial typography and summary SVGs. Backoffice headings use the system sans family alongside Tabler form controls, buttons, badges, cards, navigation and tables. Prefer clear labels and spacing over ornament or motion.

Brand palette: paper #f6f5f0; ink #18322f; primary #1b514b; primary hover #123d38; secondary brass #99672d; muted #52645f; rules #dbe2dd. Error/danger remains red, semantically separate from primary. Dark-mode equivalents must preserve contrasts and data-series distinction.
